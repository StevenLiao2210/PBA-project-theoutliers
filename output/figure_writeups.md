# Figure 1: Minimum-stay requirements
Of 6,366 Taipei listings with valid minimum-stay rules, 3,210 (50.4%) allow stays of 1-2 nights, 1,189 (18.7%) require 3-6 nights, and 1,967 (30.9%) require at least 7 nights. Requirements cluster at 1 night (1,769 listings), 2 nights (1,441), and 30 nights (935). There are 1,438 listings requiring at least 28 nights. These counts suggest meaningful variation between short-stay and longer-stay offerings, rather than one minimum-stay rule shared by the whole sample.

This variation makes our research question worth asking: do listings targeting longer stays also have more stable seasonal prices? Each proposed group contains over a thousand listings before price filtering. A future analysis would compare the same listing across periods and relate its percentage price difference to these groups, accounting for neighbourhood, room type, and guest capacity. The broad 7+ group combines weekly and monthly policies; we would check whether separating them changes the descriptive pattern.

# Figure 2: Quoted nightly prices
Among 5,867 listings with a positive, currency-verified TWD price, median quoted nightly prices are TWD 2,798 for 1-2-night requirements, TWD 2,730 for 3-6 nights, and TWD 1,543 for 7+ nights. The boxes show the middle 50% of prices; the distributions overlap and include a long upper tail. The logarithmic axis keeps unusually expensive listings visible without compressing the main distributions. Of the original 6,367 listings, 499 lack a usable price and one further listing lacks a valid minimum-stay rule. No otherwise eligible positive TWD prices are trimmed.

The spread and overlap motivate a paired comparison and attention to skewness, rather than treating a difference in price levels as evidence of seasonal stability. These are quotes for differing check-in dates and stay lengths; prices may incorporate long-stay discounts. This cross-sectional figure therefore does not measure a December-versus-August premium or establish that minimum stays cause price differences.

# Feasibility limitation
Feasibility finding: the supplied calendar has 2,323,955 listing-date rows for 6,367 listings, covering September 28, 2026 to September 28, 2027, but only listing ID, date, availability, and minimum/maximum nights. It has no price column. Thus the planned December 2026 versus August 2027 comparison cannot be calculated from these files. We must obtain date-level advertised prices or revise the question before the main analysis. Availability is not a price or a reliable measure of bookings; unavailable dates can be host-blocked.
